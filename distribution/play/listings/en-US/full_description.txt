Living Scroll is a game master's companion for tabletop role-playing games. Write your adventure once, then run it at the table — scene by scene, choice by choice.

WRITE YOUR ADVENTURE
• Scenes linked into a branching story: start, standard, recurring and ending scenes
• Coloured story paths that group scenes into plot threads
• Non-player characters with portraits, descriptions, backstories and stat blocks for supported game systems
• Rich-text notes, images, key events and background art for every scene
• Soundtracks: attach music to scenes and let it play automatically
• Visibility rules that reveal notes and characters only when the right events have happened

RUN IT AT THE TABLE
• Play view built for the game master: everything the current scene needs, in one place
• Prep mode for a safe dry run — nothing is saved
• Track key events and retire characters as the story moves on
• Improvise with ad-hoc scenes and jump back into the plot whenever you like
• Split the party into parallel tracks and bring them back together
• Quick GM notes written during the session
• Progress is saved automatically, so you can pause and resume any time

REMEMBER EVERY SESSION
• Finished games are archived with their full history
• Replay a past session step by step, exactly as it was played
• Carry progress over from a finished game into the next adventure of a campaign

SHARE AND EXPORT
• Export adventures as portable archives and import them on another device
• Reuse elements (characters, notes, scenes) between adventures
• Export an adventure to a LaTeX handbook you can compile into a printable book

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Works fully offline — no account, no ads, no tracking
• Your adventures stay on your device

Available in English, German, French, Portuguese, Spanish, Polish, Chinese and Japanese. Also available for Windows and Linux at livingscroll.net.
